# Maps

A dated log of mapping experiments: land patents, township plats, and GIS layers for the Walla Walla Valley and beyond. Newest first.

## Frenchtown land patents, six-township plat
- **Status:** Paused. Southern T06N data incomplete.
- **Active:** 9–12 August 2026
- **Last updated:** 12 August 2026
- **Question:** Where did the original federal land patentees settle in and around Frenchtown, and how do those parcels relate to the modern landscape?
- **Data:** GLO land patent records for T06N–T07N, R33E–R35E, including the Oregon-side (Umatilla County) patents for the southern half of T06N; Walla Walla County section, road, and stream layers.
- **Method:**
  - Patents were merged and de-duplicated, then placed on section polygons using aliquot (quarter-section) codes.
  - Parcels are drawn solid where real geometry exists and dashed where the subdivision is estimated.
  - Individual township plates were made first, then rebuilt as one combined 24 × 36 in print sheet with a single unified grid so the township seams align.
  - Matching ArcGIS layers were produced for the six townships and for a twelve-township core area.
- **Result:** Clear cluster of Francophone/Métis patentee names in T07N R33E–R34E.
  - Known gap: sections 19–36 of T06N rely on synthetic geometry, and one page of the Oregon-side data is still missing.
  - Lesson: design large SVGs around Illustrator's import quirks before hand-finishing begins. That means trimming lines to the page, flagging transparency trade-offs, and grouping by category layer.
