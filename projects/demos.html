Demos

Public demonstration copies of four tools built with Claude in 2026. Each one runs on a small set of sample material; the full tools work on the complete sources.

Needs and goals across the four projects

  1. Absolute fidelity to the source. In particular: diplomatic transcription; period spellings, scribal conventions, and Indigenous names.
  2. Visible uncertainty. Doubt is marked, rated, and logged, never silently resolved.
  3. Manuscript access for all users, i.e. transparency vs. authority — the evolving editorial function.
  4. Build interaction tools for low-tech collaborators.
  5. Rights and credit. Make the AI help you keep it (and you!) on the right(s) side of the line.

Fort Nez Perces Post Journal

A blind machine reading of five leaves of the post journal kept by Simon McGillivray Jr. (HBCA B.146/a/2, December 1831 – January 1832). Every name, Indigenous word, and uncertain reading is flagged for a reviewer to accept, correct, or escalate against the scan.
