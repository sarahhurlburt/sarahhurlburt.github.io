How to review a page

Read each transcribed line against the scan. Click a line to highlight it on the scan and in the translation. Click an underlined word (a flag) to open its card, then accept, correct or escalate it. If you spot an error the machine did not flag, select the line and choose Log unflagged error. Click a translation line twice to correct it.

Every decision becomes a row in the review sheet at the bottom of the page, as it would in the reviewers' spreadsheet. Click a row to return to that place; Remove undoes it. Nothing is saved: reloading the page starts over.

Each test-batch page carries a difficulty tier: A, fair copies and clean letters; B, working drafts with revisions; C, faded or cross-written letters and other hands.
