What you are looking at

Eleven photographs, fronts and backs, from the Bernice Irene Lavadour Fitzpatrick loan (2024-L-005), with the working notes from the cataloguing spreadsheet as they stood partway through the work. Titles and dates are still mostly blank: this is the stage where the tool does its job.

Try this

  • Read the back with Verso, then write a title from what it says.
  • Some backs were scanned sideways: rotate them, or click to zoom in on the handwriting.
  • Change Status and watch the badge update.
  • Switch to Grid, mark a few cards “keep”, then open Album to lay them out as printable pages.

Shortcuts

← → previous / next record · F flip front and back · Esc reset zoom. Shortcuts pause while you type in a field.
