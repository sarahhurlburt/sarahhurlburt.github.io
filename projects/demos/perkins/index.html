6th Sheet — Destroyed Before Sending

Perkins noted in his closing (ms. pg. 57): “The 6th Sheet I have thrown into the fire.” These four pages (ms. pgs. 21–24) cannot be recovered. They likely continued the ethnographic description of burial customs ending the 5th Sheet and resuming on the 7th.
